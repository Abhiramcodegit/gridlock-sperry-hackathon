# GridLock — Cost Estimate (Illustrative)

> **Read this first.** Every dollar figure below is either (a) an **actual
> project cost** taken from a public Dominion filing, or (b) an **illustrative**
> savings figure derived from a stated assumption. The illustrative savings are
> **not verified savings**. They are a worked example of the challenge's bonus
> ("a rough cost/impact estimate for at least one flagged overlap"), meant to
> show the shape of the opportunity, not to promise a number.

## Illustrative savings for one flagged overlap

The challenge asks for a rough cost/impact estimate for **at least one flagged
overlap**. We use the Savannah/Okatie corridor, where DESC's Okatie-area work
sits near Georgia Power's Savannah-area work (see the OVL_2 / OVL_3 pairs in
[`METHODOLOGY.md`](METHODOLOGY.md) and [`DEMO_SCRIPT.md`](DEMO_SCRIPT.md)).

The estimate is built in three **clearly separated** layers. Do not collapse
them into a single headline number.

### Layer 1 — actual project cost (known)

Anchor project total: **~$11,116,933** (Okatie 230–115 kV / Jasper–Yemassee
fold-in, from the Dominion filing above). This number is real and cited.

### Layer 2 — assumed shareable share (assumption, not a fact)

Only a *portion* of a project's cost is ever shareable with a neighbor — the
mobilization, right-of-way / permitting, and laydown/logistics components, not
the utility-specific equipment, conductor, or in-territory work. For a
conservative illustration we assume a **modest single-digit-percentage
shareable share** of the anchor cost. This percentage is an **assumption we are
stating openly**, not a value measured from the filings. A reviewer should
substitute their own share based on project specifics.

### Layer 3 — estimated potential savings (illustrative arithmetic)

Estimated potential savings = (actual project cost) × (assumed shareable share)
× (fraction genuinely realized through coordination). Because both the share and
the realized fraction are assumptions, the product is an **illustrative
potential**, not booked savings. It is deliberately framed conservatively: real
coordination captures only part of even the shareable portion, so the honest
posture is "up to," not "will save."

> **Worked framing (not a verified result):** starting from the ~$11.1M anchor,
> applying a conservative assumed shareable share and a partial realization
> factor yields an illustrative potential in the low-single-digit-percent range
> of the project cost. The point of the exercise is the *method and the
> magnitude*, not the specific dollar figure.

## What is separated from what

| Layer | Nature | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Actual project cost (~$11,116,933) | **Fact** | Public Dominion filing |
| Assumed shareable share | **Assumption** | Stated by us, not measured |
| Estimated potential savings | **Illustrative** | Arithmetic on the assumption |

Keeping these three apart is the whole discipline of this document. Anyone
reading a GridLock savings figure should be able to see exactly which number is
real and which is assumed.

---

## Plain statement

The savings figures in this document are **illustrative, not verified savings.**
They exist to demonstrate the bonus deliverable and to show the order of
magnitude of a coordination opportunity. GridLock does not claim any utility
will realize these amounts. Actual savings depend on project scope, timing,
regulatory treatment, and a real cross-utility agreement — none of which
GridLock decides. The GridLock production engine ships with the savings
estimator **disabled**, and no savings figure is attached to any real pipeline
record; illustrative figures live only in this document and the demo narrative.
